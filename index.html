Odds and rules

Dust 60%, Ember 25%, Nebula 10%, Pulsar 4%, Singularity 1%. A Pulsar or better is guaranteed within 25 opens.

Every relic also rolls an erosion value from 0 to 1, and lower is better. Unmarked 5%, Lustrous 15%, Aged 25%, Cracked 30%, Fading 25%.

One drop in 20 is a variant: Gilded 2%, Haunted 1.5%, Echo 1%, Inverted 0.5%. Every drop also gets a pattern number from 0000 to 9999.

Shards come from selling relics in the Market (a 10% fee applies), filling the daily bounties, and a small alms when you are nearly out. Relic value depends on tier, erosion, variant and pattern, and the Sky shifts prices every 3 minutes: one tier is ascendant (+50%) and another is waning (−25%).

Market events (meteor showers, crashes, collector crazes, variant fever) and collector offers run on a timer shared by every player. The Eclipse Case is a weekly rotating case with four exclusive relics and its own constellation.

The daily gauntlet uses a seed shared by everyone each day. Finish it for a shard bonus that grows with your streak. Finish constellations to unlock chapters of the Chronicle.
